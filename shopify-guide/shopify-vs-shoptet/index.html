Shopify vs Shoptet

Two hosted platforms with different priorities. Shoptet starts from local operations. Shopify focuses on a global ecosystem, a customisable storefront and international expansion.

AuthorJakub HrnčířReviewed13 Aug 2026Reading time7 minutes

There is no universal answer to “Shopify or Shoptet?”. Both host and maintain the technical core of an online store, but they reduce different types of work. Shoptet offers a direct route to common Czech and Slovak requirements. Shopify gives brands broader options for tailored customer experience and multiple markets. The decision should therefore start with operational processes rather than a feature count.

2026 pricing: compare more than subscriptions

As reviewed on 13 August 2026, Shopify lists Basic from CZK 499 per month with annual billing on its Czech pricing page. Shoptet lists Basic at CZK 440 per month excluding VAT with monthly billing and also offers a limited free plan for up to ten products. These prices use different billing assumptions and feature sets, so comparing 499 with 440 in isolation is misleading.

The real budget includes the theme, paid apps or add-ons, development, data connections, payment fees and ongoing support. Shopify lists third-party transaction fees according to plan when an external payment provider is used; the structure changes when Shopify Payments is available and selected. Shoptet states that it does not charge commission on store turnover. Always check live pricing before making a decision.

Czech payments, shipping and accounting

Shoptet benefits from being built around the regional market. Many common Czech payments, carriers and operational workflows sit within its ecosystem and its administration matches local merchant expectations. This can shorten the path to launch for a standard Czech store.

Shopify can also support Czech operations, but shipping, invoicing, accounting, product feeds and returns should be checked individually. Its global App Store and custom integrations offer breadth, while the trade-off can be more implementation decisions and greater reliance on apps.

Design, themes and custom functionality

Shopify themes use Liquid and support custom sections, apps and APIs. That suits D2C brands looking for a stronger visual identity, deliberate mobile UX and continuous development. Shopify still has platform rules: checkout extensibility, APIs and advanced features vary by plan.

Shoptet gives merchants a more predefined framework. That is useful for a conventional catalogue and purchase flow. For unusual product logic or a heavily tailored interface, confirm whether a template, add-on, Shoptet Premium or individual development can cover the requirement before committing.

When expansion changes the answer

For a store focused mainly on Czechia or Slovakia, local simplicity may matter more than global reach. When a brand needs multiple languages, currencies, domains, catalogues or market-specific pricing, Shopify Markets provides a shared framework. It does not replace tax, legal or logistics advice.

Who each platform is for

  • Shoptet: a local store with standard operations that values Czech administration and ready regional connections.
  • Shopify: a brand prioritising design, mobile experience, custom development or gradual international expansion.
  • Analyse first: B2B, complex ERP, multiple warehouses, unusual pricing or custom purchase logic.
Do not choose the platform that can theoretically do more. Choose the one that creates less friction for the way you actually sell.

Frequently asked questions

Is Shopify or Shoptet better for a Czech store?

Shoptet is often the shorter route for a standard local operation. Shopify is a strong candidate for brands prioritising a tailored storefront, international growth and a global ecosystem. The right choice depends on the project.

Is Shopify more expensive than Shoptet?

Plan prices alone are not enough. Compare apps, add-ons, development, payment fees and support over at least two to three years.

Can a Shoptet store migrate to Shopify?

Yes. A controlled migration needs a plan for products, customers, orders, URLs, SEO, integrations, analytics and test orders.
