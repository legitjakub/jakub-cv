Shopify Plus for Czech Stores in 2026: Features and Cost

Current overview of price, B2B functions, multiple stores and local operation. No shortcuts and no assumption that the highest plan is automatically the best choice.

AuthorJakub HrnčířUpdated28 July 2026Reading time8 minutes

Shopify Plus is Shopify's highest plan, designed for stores that need more advanced multi-store management, larger B2B scenarios, higher operational capacity, or specific checkout adjustments. However, a higher turnover in itself is not a reason to switch. The decisive factor is whether the store will actually use the functions that the lower plan does not provide.

What changed in 2026

The difference between Shopify Plus and lower plans has changed during 2026. Shopify has also made basic B2B functions available on the Basic, Grow and Advanced plans. Company profiles, payment terms, self-service orders, quantity rules and a limited number of catalogs are therefore no longer a reason for Plus in themselves.

Plus retains more advanced options, such as unlimited B2B catalogs, direct catalog assignment to a specific company or branch, and selected advance and partial payment scenarios. At the same time, Shopify Scripts ended on June 30, 2026. Stores with original custom logic must use corresponding apps or solutions based on Shopify Functions.

The Spring '26 Edition also brought broader support for agentic commerce and AI sales channel management. It is a new distribution channel, not a replacement for quality product data, a usable online store or regular SEO.

Neither Checkout Extensibility nor Shopify Functions are simple toggles that automatically solve every input. The availability of a particular extension depends on the type of function, the method of deployment and the plan. Therefore, the proposal needs to be verified against the current documentation before the project is estimated.

How much does Shopify Plus cost?

As of July 28, 2026, Shopify lists the price for European Union countries EUR 2,250 per month with a one-year contract a EUR 2,100 per month on a three-year contract. For larger or more complex businesses, the offer may include a variable platform fee depending on turnover and business model. The final terms must be confirmed directly with Shopify, as the pricing page and terms and conditions are subject to change.

The plan is only one part of the total cost. The budget typically includes design, theme development, data migration, app, integration, testing, theme licensing for individual production stores, and subsequent technical management.

  • Check the price and contract length directly in the current Shopify Plus pricing page
  • List all production stores and verify their eligibility as expansion stores
  • Factor in separate theme licenses and any app fees for each store
  • Separate one-time implementation from regular operating costs
  • Get payment fees and any individual conditions confirmed in writing

Czech and Slovak requirements

Shopify Payments is currently available for eligible businesses in the Czech Republic. Slovakia is not on the official list of supported countries, therefore the Slovak store chooses an external provider available directly in the Shopify admin. The availability of specific payment methods, carriers, invoicing or ERP connections always needs to be verified for a specific store, country and operational process.

Plus itself does not provide local transport, accounting or product integrations. These can be solved by a ready-made app, middleware or custom integration. It is important to describe the source of the data, the responsibility of the individual systems, the behavior in the event of an error and the method of subsequent support.

When Plus makes sense

  • Yes: you need advanced B2B functions that are not available on the lower plan
  • Yes: you operate multiple eligible stores and use organizational management Plus
  • Yes: a specific request for checkout, API or custom Functions has been technically validated
  • Rather not: the main issue is content, mobile interface, speed or unclear customer journey
  • Rather not: the decision is based solely on turnover or the expectation that a higher plan will automatically increase conversions
Shopify Plus is an investment in operational capabilities. It has value only if it solves a specific limitation and the benefit outweighs the cost of the plan and implementation.

Frequently asked questions

Is Shopify B2B only available on Plus?

No. From 2026, basic B2B functions are also available on the Basic, Grow and Advanced plans. Plus adds, for example, unlimited catalogs, direct company catalogs and selected advanced payment options.

How much does Shopify Plus cost in the Czech Republic?

As of July 28, 2026, Shopify for EU countries is quoting €2,250 per month on a one-year contract and €2,100 per month on a three-year contract. Individual terms and conditions may apply to more complex and larger transactions.

Are there more stores included in Plus?

A standard Plus contract supports a main store and up to nine eligible expansion stores. Stores work independently, they don't automatically share data, and each production store needs its own paid theme license.

Is Plus worth it because of higher turnover?

Turnover is only one entry. More important is whether the business will use the specific features, limits and organizational tools of Plus and whether their benefits will outweigh the price of the platform and implementation.
