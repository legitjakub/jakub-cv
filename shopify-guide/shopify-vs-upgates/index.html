Shopify vs Upgates

Both are hosted solutions, but they come from different environments. Upgates focuses on ready-made features for local online stores. Shopify offers a global commerce ecosystem and broader extensibility.

AuthorJakub HrnčířReviewed13 August 2026Reading time6 minutes

Upgates and Shopify both remove the need to operate your own commerce server, but they address different starting points. Upgates is a locally focused hosted solution with features designed for Czech and Slovak merchants. Shopify is a global platform with an extensive ecosystem of apps, themes, partners and international selling tools. Neither option is automatically better.

Quick answer

Upgates can be the shorter route to local operations with many functions included in the plan. Shopify more often suits a brand prioritising a tailored storefront, a global ecosystem and multiple international markets. Compare the complete operating model, not only the monthly subscription.

Plans and costs in 2026

As of 13 August 2026, the official Upgates overview lists Bronze at CZK 450, Silver at CZK 1,150, Gold at CZK 1,750 and Platinum at CZK 3,250 per month, all excluding VAT. Upgates also states that its standard plans include the available features and differ primarily in limits for products, languages, users and storage. Its individual Exclusive plan starts at CZK 10,000 per month excluding VAT according to the same source.

the Czech Shopify pricing page lists Basic from CZK 499 per month with annual billing, Grow from CZK 1,370 and Advanced from CZK 7,130. These figures cannot be compared line by line: the platforms apply different limits, billing structures and service models. Apps, additional services, payment fees, design, development and support also belong in the total budget.

Local operations and built-in features

Upgates can be practical for a merchant that wants a Czech environment and many common functions inside one system. Its local context is easier to understand. Still, verify the exact payment providers, carriers, accounting, warehouse processes and data connections before choosing it. A general feature-list item may not describe your specific operating scenario.

Shopify offers a broad global App Store and custom integrations. This provides more alternatives, but it can also increase the number of separate apps and vendors. A good implementation therefore considers not only whether a function exists, but also its cost, performance, data handling and long-term dependency.

Design and brand development

Shopify is strong for projects where the storefront and mobile customer experience are priorities. A theme can be extended with Liquid, custom sections and JavaScript while keeping content manageable for the brand team.

Upgates provides its own template system and visual editing tools. The prepared framework can be efficient for a standard store. For a highly specific design, interaction or product workflow, check the technical boundaries and the availability of an experienced supplier in advance.

Languages, domains and expansion

Upgates varies the number of languages by plan and lists multidomain operation as an additional service. Shopify handles international configuration through Markets, including markets, domains, languages, currencies, catalogues and local pricing where available. With either platform, tax, legal, logistics and customer-support requirements remain separate business responsibilities.

Which platform fits which situation?

  • Upgates: a Czech or Slovak store that wants a broad set of local features in a hosted solution and values Czech administration.
  • Shopify: a brand prioritising a custom storefront, global apps, continuous development or several international markets.
  • Verify first: specific ERP and B2B workflows, unusual checkout requirements, language count and the cost of essential connections.
The key question is not how many features a platform lists. It is whether the platform supports your actual process without expensive workarounds.

Frequently asked questions

Is Upgates suitable for a Czech online store?

Upgates is a locally focused hosted solution with Czech administration, built-in features and plans differentiated mainly by product, language, user and storage limits. Suitability still depends on the store's actual processes.

When does Shopify make more sense?

Shopify is often a strong candidate for brands that need a customisable storefront, a global app ecosystem and a long-term framework for multiple international markets.

Can a store migrate from Upgates to Shopify?

Yes, but the available exports for products, variants, customers and orders need to be checked first, followed by URL mapping, integrations, analytics and launch testing.
