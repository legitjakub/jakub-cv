Shopify vs Shoptet vs WooCommerce for CZ/SK Stores

The right platform is not the one with the longest list of features. It is the one that corresponds to the sales model, the team, the local needs and the ambitions of the project.

AuthorJakub HrnčířUpdated29 July 2026Reading time5 minutes

Shopify, Shoptet and WooCommerce can run a quality online store. The difference is not whether you can sell on the platform, but how easy the particular solution is to set up, develop and maintain. Therefore, the comparison only makes sense when you know the priorities of your project.

When does Shopify make sense?

Shopify is a strong choice for a brand that wants a stable hosted platform, a strong customer-facing storefront, the ability to develop its own sections and access to a wide ecosystem of apps. It is more prominent with D2C brands, international projects and stores that do not want to deal with server infrastructure internally.

It is necessary to take into account that some Czech and Slovak needs are solved by integration or app. Therefore, check the shipping, payment, accounting, product channels and invoicing method before choosing. Shopify is not automatically an expensive or cheap solution; cost depends on how many custom features the project needs.

When does Shoptet make sense?

Shoptet is a natural choice for a store focused primarily on the CZ/SK market, which wants a quick start, a familiar local environment and a large part of common integrations prepared for the region. For many smaller and medium-sized online stores, it can be easier than an international platform.

The question comes when the brand needs a very specific design, an unusual product logic or a significantly different buying process. Then it is advisable to check the limits of the available modifications in advance.

When does WooCommerce make sense?

WooCommerce is suitable for teams that want high control over WordPress, content, hosting and source code. A great advantage can be the flexibility and connection of the content website with the store. But the same freedom means responsibility for updates, security, plugin compatibility and performance.

Therefore, the quality of a WooCommerce project strongly depends on the chosen technical solution and the people who take care of it. A cheap start does not necessarily mean cheap long-term operation, just as self-hosting does not automatically guarantee better performance.

How B2C, D2C and B2B affect the decision

For classic B2C, the speed of Shopify admin, campaigns and local logistics are important. A D2C brand usually puts more emphasis on design, content, customer experience and expansion. A B2B project may require companies, individual price lists, order approval or ERP integration. Choose a platform based on these processes, not a general ranking.

  • List markets, languages, currencies and local transport
  • Separate must-have features from "future" ideas
  • Validate data and accounting integrations before signing the project
  • Calculate the three-year run, not just the start-up cost
  • Consider who will manage and develop the online store internally

Practical verdict

For a local and standardized online store, Shoptet can be the shortest way. WooCommerce may be suitable for a content-rich website with its own technical management. For a brand that wants a stable business platform, a customizable customer section, and room for international growth, Shopify tends to be a strong candidate.

The choice of platform is correct if it reduces long-term friction in operation. Not when he wins the most points in the general table.

Frequently asked questions

Which platform is best for a smaller Czech online store?

There is no universal winner. The degree of customization required, local operation, budget, technical management capability and potential expansion plan are key.

Can the platform be changed later?

Yes, but migration means working with data, site addresses, integrations, measurement and traffic. The more custom features a store uses, the more important it is to prepare for the transition.

Will Shopify handle the needs of the Czech and Slovak market?

Many requirements can be addressed by setup, app or integration. However, the specific shipping, payments, accounting and other local processes need to be verified according to the actual business specification.
