Shopify Scripts have ended: moving to Functions

Shopify Scripts stopped executing on 30 June 2026. If a store handled custom discounts, shipping rates or B2B pricing through them, that logic simply disappeared. What replaces it, and what do you need to know first?

AuthorJakub HrnčířUpdated22 July 2026Reading time6 minutes

This is not an article about new features, but about something that has already happened and that a share of stores still has not resolved. Shopify Scripts were switched off on 30 June 2026. From 15 April 2026 they could no longer be edited or published, and from the end of June they stopped executing entirely.

If a store used Scripts, their logic simply stopped applying on that date. In practice this shows up quietly — checkout still works, but a discount is not applied, a free gift is not added, or a wholesale customer sees the regular price.

How to find out what broke

Shopify provides a report in the admin listing the customizations that were active in the store before Scripts were deprecated. For each item it links to the matching Shopify Functions documentation or to an app that does the same job. That is the fastest starting point — before writing any code.

Alongside the report it is worth reviewing recent orders and checking whether discounts, shipping and business-customer pricing came out correctly. A missing discount is easier to spot in the data than in the interface.

What Shopify Functions are

Shopify Functions let you customise Shopify's backend logic — much like Scripts did, but in a newer and more stable model. The key difference is that Functions are deployed as part of an app, rather than as an isolated script in the admin.

They can be written in any language that compiles to WebAssembly. Shopify directly supports and provides templates for Rust, which it describes as the most performant choice, and for JavaScript.

DiscountCustom discount types and discount rules
PaymentHiding or adjusting payment options at checkout
Delivery optionsCustomising the delivery choices offered

Plan requirements: when Plus is needed

Here is the nuance that often gets missed in discussions, and that determines the budget:

  • A public App Store app containing Functions works on any plan
  • A custom app with Functions requires the Shopify Plus plan
  • Some specific Functions capabilities are available only on Plus

In practice this means a store on Grow or Advanced that previously had custom discount logic in Scripts cannot simply rewrite it into a custom app — it has to either use a suitable App Store app, adjust business rules so native discounts cover them, or consider moving to Plus.

How to proceed

01
Establish the scope

The admin report plus a review of recent orders — what exactly stopped applying.

02
Choose the route

Native discounts, an App Store app, or a custom Function — depending on plan and rule complexity.

03
Deploy and test

Verify against real combinations of cart contents, customer segments and payment methods.

04

Compare orders before and after deployment to confirm the rules calculate correctly.

The riskiest option is doing nothing. A missing discount or a wrong wholesale price shows up in revenue, not in an error message — and it can run for weeks before anyone notices.

Frequently asked questions

When exactly did Shopify Scripts stop working?

From 15 April 2026 Scripts could no longer be edited or published, and on 30 June 2026 they stopped executing entirely. Published Scripts were deactivated on that date, so their logic no longer applies in the store.

How do I find out what our Scripts were doing?

Shopify provides a report in the admin listing the customizations that were active in the store before Scripts were deprecated, including links to the matching Shopify Functions documentation or apps that can restore the functionality.

Do Shopify Functions require the Plus plan?

It depends on how they are deployed. Functions delivered by a public App Store app work on any plan. Custom apps containing Shopify Functions, however, require the Shopify Plus plan, and some specific Functions capabilities are available only on Plus.

Which language are Shopify Functions written in?

Any language that compiles to WebAssembly can be used. Shopify directly supports and provides templates for Rust, which it describes as the most performant choice, and for JavaScript.

Can I just replace Scripts with an App Store app?

For common scenarios such as volume discounts or a free gift with purchase, that is usually the fastest route. You should verify how the app handles your specific rules, how it behaves when discounts combine, and what impact it has on checkout performance.
