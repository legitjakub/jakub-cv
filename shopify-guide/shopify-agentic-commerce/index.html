Agentic Commerce: AI Shopping on Shopify in 2026

More and more purchasing decisions start with a conversation with an AI assistant, not with a visit to an online store. Agentic commerce describes how AI agents browse catalogs, build a cart, and complete a purchase on behalf of a customer. How does it work and what does the online store have to do for it?

AuthorJakub HrnčířUpdated28 July 2026Reading time6 minutes

Agentic commerce was one of the main themes of Shopify Editions Spring ’26, but it describes a broader shift in how people shop online. Instead of the customer opening the online store, comparing products and going through the checkout themselves, this process can be partially or completely taken over by an AI agent in ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini or a similar tool.

The Shopify Editions Spring '26 overview covers the wider release. This article focuses on how agentic commerce works in practice and what merchants should prepare.

What is agentic commerce?

In classic e-commerce, the customer is the one who goes through the catalog, makes a decision and buys. In agentic commerce, this role is partially taken over by an AI agent — based on an input such as "find me running shoes under CZK 3,000 that will arrive by Friday", it searches available stores, compares offers and can immediately suggest or complete the purchase. At the same time, the agent works with structured data from the store, not with how the page looks visually.

Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)

Universal Commerce Protocol is an open standard that Shopify developed together with Google and which has the support of companies such as Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Salesforce, Stripe, Etsy, Target or Visa. It solves a fundamental problem: without a common standard, each AI platform (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) would require its own integration with the cart and checkout of each store separately.

UCP structures communication between an AI agent and a store across four areas:

01
Authentication

The agent identifies itself, and the store applies the relevant permissions and limits.

02
Catalogue

The agent searches for products — either across all Shopify stores or in the catalog of a specific online store.

03
Cart and checkout

The agent builds the cart and, where supported, can complete the purchase for the customer.

04
Order tracking

Delivery, claims and return information can be exchanged through APIs and webhooks.

Shopify Catalog and Agentic Storefronts in action

For online store owners, implementation is simpler than the UCP itself sounds. Shopify handles the technical side of the protocol for the store — merchants can use Agentic Storefronts in Shopify admin to manage supported AI sales channels. Product data is drawn from Shopify Catalog, a structured database that AI agents can search directly — without having to scrape the page as older tools did.

According to Shopify data for the first quarter of 2026 (year-over-year comparison to Q1 2025), the impact of AI channels looks like this:

The last line is the most important from a practical point of view: the AI agent sends the customer directly to a specific product, not to the homepage or to a category. Thus, the product detail itself must contain everything necessary to make a decision — a complete description, availability, shipping and return conditions.

For specific brands, Shopify reports, for example, 3.2% of total revenue from AI channels (Omnilux, March 2026) or 20x year-over-year revenue growth from these channels (Cozy Earth). According to Shopify, search built on the structured Shopify Catalog should convert roughly twice as well as results from scraped data.

The numbers need to be taken with a grain of salt — this is data published by Shopify itself, not independent research, and the high growth multiple comes from a low base. But they show a consistent direction across categories.

What you need to do for AI agents to find you

AI agents make decisions based on data, not an impression of the appearance of the online store. The priority should therefore be:

  • Complete and accurate product data — names, descriptions, variants, images and categories without gaps and inconsistencies
  • Current availability — warehouse, delivery times and delivery options must correspond to reality, the agent compares deals precisely according to the reliability of this data
  • Clear terms — returns, shipping and payments available in a structured way via API, not just in subpage text
  • Active involvement in Agentic Storefronts — mere presence on Shopify is not enough, visibility in AI channels needs to be explicitly turned on

This is closely intertwined with the concept that has recently been called Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) — an SEO equivalent focused on visibility in AI answers and recommendations, not just in classic search results.

Risks and open questions

Agentic commerce is still an early topic and it is worth approaching it objectively, not as an automatic guarantee of higher sales:

  • Adoption among customers is just starting — the volume of purchases through AI agents is still one percent for most stores
  • Data protection and security issues remain open when authorizing payments on behalf of the customer
  • Not every type of product or business model lends itself equally well to AI-mediated purchasing — for products where personal testing or advice is decisive, the role of the agent will be more complementary
  • Control over the presentation of the brand is lower than when the customer browses the online store directly — the choice is largely determined by the data structure, not the visual identity

Do you need Shopify Plus for this?

Not necessarily. Both UCP and Shopify Catalog are available across plans, and as of the Spring '26 Edition, the protocol is open to all developers with no approval required. Shopify Plus makes more sense for large catalogs, higher volumes of data and advanced implementation support — not as a condition for access to agentic commerce itself.

Agentic commerce does not favor big brands, but shops with good data. A smaller online store with a clean catalog has a real chance to be seen just like the enterprise competition.

Frequently asked questions

What is agentic commerce?

It is a shopping journey mediated by an AI agent. The agent can search a catalogue, build a cart and, where supported, complete a purchase for the customer inside an interface such as ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini.

Is Universal Commerce Protocol only available for Shopify Plus?

No. Both UCP and Shopify Catalog are available across plans and open to all developers starting with the Spring '26 Edition without approval. Shopify Plus mainly adds value with larger catalogs, higher volumes, and advanced support, not protocol access alone.

How do I enable Agentic Storefronts?

Directly in the Shopify admin with a switch for individual AI channels (ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot). Shopify then handles the technical side of the protocol itself; the condition is to have complete and accurate product data in the catalog.

Does agentic commerce replace classic SEO?

It does not replace, rather complements. Data structure, product descriptions and technical SEO fundamentals remain important even for AI agents to correctly understand and recommend the store.

Are customers converting better from AI channels?

According to Shopify data for Q1 2026, visits from AI assistants convert roughly 50% better than visits from organic search and have a 14% higher average order value. Visits from these channels grew more than eight times year-on-year, orders almost thirteen times.
