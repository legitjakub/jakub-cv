How Much Does a Shopify Store Cost in 2026?

The Shopify plan is only the starting point. The full budget depends on the theme, custom features, data, integrations, the quality of the brief and ongoing development after launch.

AuthorJakub HrnčířUpdated13 August 2026Reading time5 minutes

There is no single honest answer to “How much does a Shopify store cost?” The same platform can serve a small catalogue or a brand selling across several countries with warehouse integrations and a tailored B2B process. A useful estimate therefore starts with the project scope, not a developer's rate card.

Quick answer

As of 13 August 2026, annual billing prices in Czechia are CZK 499 per month for Basic, CZK 1,370 for Grow and CZK 7,130 for Advanced. Shopify Plus starts at EUR 2,250 per month on a one-year term or EUR 2,100 on a three-year term. The total budget has three separate layers: platform, implementation and ongoing operation.

1. Regular platform costs

Shopify works like a subscription. The plan includes hosting, basic Shopify admin, infrastructure and the continuously developed core of the platform. The amount of the subscription varies depending on the selected plan and billing period. In addition to the plan, you also need to take into account fees related to payments and any paid apps.

As of 13 August 2026, the official Czech Shopify pricing page lists annual-billing prices of CZK 499 per month for Basic, CZK 1,370 for Grow and CZK 7,130 for Advanced. Shopify Plus starts at EUR 2,250 per month on a one-year term or EUR 2,100 on a three-year term. Prices, taxes and terms can change, so verify them before making a decision.

2. What determines the implementation cost

The biggest differences arise between a project built on a ready-made theme and a store that needs its own design, sections, logic or integrations. Both options can be correct. What matters is whether the solution supports the business objective and whether it will be reasonably manageable.

01
Project brief and information architecture

Products, collections, navigation, languages, markets, content and the order process should be clarified before development.

02
Theme or custom design

A ready-made theme can shorten the launch. A distinctive brand or an unusual purchase journey usually requires more customization.

03
Features and integrations

Filtering, product configurators, shipping, ERP, email marketing and B2B logic each have different technical requirements.

04
Data, testing and launch

Product imports, URL redirects, analytics and test orders are essential parts of a controlled launch.

3. Shopify agency, developer, or internal team?

A large Shopify agency makes sense if the project simultaneously requires strategy, UX, design, development, integration, project management and long-term support from a larger team. An independent Shopify developer may be more efficient for clearly defined development, redesign, Liquid theme modifications, or collaboration with an existing in-house or agency team.

An in-house developer makes sense when there is a consistent workload and technical decision-making is part of daily operations. External collaboration is often more flexible for smaller or irregular volumes of work. What matters most is clear ownership, effective communication and the ability to turn the brief into a maintainable result.

4. Post-launch costs

The project does not end with launch. Expect content updates, campaign tweaks, new sections, app reviews, continuous mobile UX improvements, and troubleshooting that real data will show. Support can work in the form of individual tasks or a regular range of hours.

  • Continuous modifications of the theme and custom sections
  • App and integration compatibility check
  • Performance, Core Web Vitals and Mobile Usability
  • Expansion into additional languages or markets
  • Technical support for campaigns and new features

5. How to get a usable estimate

For a first rough estimate, just describe the current platform, number of products and variants, target countries, desired features, existing design and expected deadline. The more accurate the input, the fewer reserves and unknowns the estimate must contain.

A good offer doesn't just explain the amount. It shows what will be created, what remains out of scope, and what decisions can change the price.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to run Shopify itself?

Regular costs include the selected plan, domain, any paid apps and fees associated with payments. Check the current plan cost on the official Shopify pricing page.

Is it cheaper to be an independent developer or an agency?

It depends on the scope and expertise needed. A single developer can be effective for a clearly defined project, while a wider agency team makes sense where development, design, data and project management are needed simultaneously.

What should the price estimate include?

In addition to development, it should include design scope, content, data migration, app, integration, testing, launch, and follow-up support. It is also important to clearly name what is not included in the price.
