Agentic commerce
on Shopify, without the fog.

AI agents in ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini or right in the browser can already search Shopify stores, and on some channels they can pay too. This page shows how that shopping journey works, what is available today and what it means for a store in Czechia or the wider EU.

Store data

  1. Step 1 of 6The shopper sets a task

    I’m looking for whole-bean espresso coffee under CZK 400. It needs to arrive by Friday.

    The shopper doesn’t type into the store’s search box but talks to an agent. The agent pulls the criteria out of the sentence: product type, brew method, price and delivery date.

    What you control: Nothing. This happens outside your store, before the agent knows you exist.

    Technical detail

    An agent is a model with access to tools. The platform decides which tools: ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Meta or an agent running in the browser. For Shopify purchases, the channels in Agentic Storefronts use Shopify’s catalog.

  2. Step 2 of 6The agent searches the catalog

    I found three espresso coffees under CZK 400. Let me check stock and delivery.

    • Demo RoasteryBrazil Cerrado, 250 gCZK 329
    • Another storeEthiopia Sidamo, 250 gCZK 389
    • Another storeColombia Huila, 250 gCZK 359

    The agent searches Shopify Catalog: a structured view of products from the stores connected to it. It gets titles, prices, variants, images and availability rather than a scraped web page.

    What you control: Titles, categories, variants and product properties. The agent can’t filter on what isn’t in the data.

    Technical detail

    Shopify offers two catalogs: Global Catalog searches across Shopify stores, Storefront Catalog within a single store. Agents connect through MCP servers that follow the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP).

    search_catalog({
  query: "whole bean espresso",
  max_price: 400, currency: "CZK"
})

    Simplified illustration, not a real API call.

  3. Step 3 of 6The agent checks the details

    Demo Roastery: in stock, delivered by Thursday, 14-day returns. I recommend it.

    Demo Roastery doesn’t state a return policy, so I can’t confirm it. I recommend a coffee from another store.

    Before recommending anything, the agent reads the product detail and facts about the store: shipping, returns, terms. In Agentic Storefronts the store’s knowledge base serves this too.

    What you control: Completed store policies and the knowledge base. Without return, privacy and terms policies a store doesn’t get into AI channels at all.

    Technical detail

    A tool such as get_product returns the product detail. The store knowledge base (Fall ’26 Edition) collects the policies and FAQ answers the merchant approves, and Shopify suggests what to add.

    get_product({ id: "brazil-cerrado-250" })
→ stock, shipping, returns

    Simplified illustration, not a real API call.

  4. Step 4 of 6The agent builds a cart

    I’ve added Brazil Cerrado, 250 g. CZK 329 plus shipping.

    The cart lives on Shopify’s side, not in the agent’s head. Prices, discounts, taxes and shipping are calculated by the store’s own rules, as in any other purchase.

    What you control: Your pricing, discount and shipping rules. Since 9 Oct 2026 Shopify Catalog also shows compare-at prices to agents; you can turn the sharing off in the admin.

    Technical detail

    Cart MCP builds and updates the cart, including lines, localisation and buyer context. Checkout MCP then turns the same cart into a checkout.

    create_cart({
  lines: [{ variant: "…-250g", qty: 1 }],
  country: "CZ"
})

    Simplified illustration, not a real API call.

    To finish, I’ll send you to Demo Roastery’s checkout.

    You can pay by card right here in Copilot.

    You can finish the purchase right in Google, if direct checkout has reached you yet.

    I’ve filled in the checkout in your open tab. Please review it and confirm the order.

    Where the shopper pays depends on the channel. ChatGPT sends them to the store’s checkout; Copilot, Google and Meta can take payment inside the conversation. A browser agent fills in the checkout in the open tab, but a person has to confirm the order.

    What you control: Which channels and direct checkout are on, under Sales channels > Agentic. If you turn direct checkout off for a channel, the shopper completes the purchase in your checkout.

    Technical detail

    Server-side agents use Checkout MCP. An agent running in the buyer’s browser can, since 28 Sep 2026, use Checkout WebMCP: the get_checkout, update_checkout and complete_checkout tools run inside checkout and submit only after the buyer confirms. 3-D Secure and similar steps always hand control back to the buyer.

    WebMCP is still a proposed standard. Chrome is testing it in an origin trial from version 149, so this is an experiment, not a finished channel.

  6. Step 6 of 6After the purchase

    Your order is confirmed. I’ll let you know when the roastery ships it.

    With these settings the purchase through an agent didn’t complete. The shopper can still find the product and buy it on the store’s website.

    The order shows up in the Shopify admin like any other. The agent can follow up on delivery, returns or refunds.

    What you control: Fulfilment, tracking and returns. What the shopper experiences after the purchase is still yours.

    Technical detail

    Order MCP returns the current order state through get_order, and webhooks report fulfilment, returns, refunds and edits. According to Shopify, Shop MCP has no webhooks yet, so agents have to poll for status.

    get_order({ id: "…" })

Who does what

Five terms,
five roles.

Articles mix these names up. Each one actually covers a different layer, and only some of them concern the merchant directly.

Data

Shopify Catalog

Available

A structured view of products from Shopify stores that AI channels and custom agents read from.

Concerns you directly. Your store’s data goes into Catalog; Fall ’26 added an eligibility check that shows what to fix.

Shopify Help Center: Agentic storefronts · Shopify Editions Fall ’26

Channels

Agentic Storefronts

Available, depending on shopper country

The part of the Shopify admin (Sales channels > Agentic) where you decide which AI channels get your data and direct checkout.

Concerns you directly. It is on by default for eligible stores, but the channels currently target shoppers outside the EU.

Shopify Help Center: Agentic storefronts

Connection

MCP

Open protocol

Model Context Protocol: a general way for an AI model to call tools and read data. Not specific to commerce.

Indirectly. Agents connect to Shopify over MCP; since 13 Oct 2026 Shopify also offers a hosted Shop MCP.

Model Context Protocol · Shopify.dev Changelog, 13 Oct 2026: Shop MCP

Commerce rules

UCP

Open standard

Universal Commerce Protocol: a shared language for catalog, cart, checkout, identity linking and orders. Runs over REST, MCP or A2A.

Indirectly. Shopify implements it for you; a custom agent or app works with it.

Universal Commerce Protocol · Shopify.dev: Agentic commerce

Browser

WebMCP

Experiment (origin trial)

A proposed web standard that lets a page offer tools to an agent in the browser, instead of the agent clicking blindly.

Watch for now. Shopify has used it in checkout since 28 Sep 2026; Chrome has been testing it since version 149.

Chrome for Developers, 9 Jun 2026: WebMCP origin trial · Shopify.dev Changelog, 28 Sep 2026: WebMCP for checkout

What’s new

The last
two months.

Changes from 7 September to 7 November 2026, from the official changelogs and the Shopify Editions page.

  1. 8 Sep 2026

    Meta as an AI channel

    Meta appeared among the AI channels in the admin. Products are shared with it through Shopify Catalog by default. Shopify Changelog, 8 Sep 2026: Meta as an AI channel

  2. 28 Sep 2026

    WebMCP in checkout

    Browser agents can read and fill in checkout in the open tab. They submit the order only after the buyer confirms, and the merchant sets nothing up. Shopify.dev Changelog, 28 Sep 2026: WebMCP for checkout

  3. 9 Oct 2026

    Compare-at prices for agents

    Shopify Catalog shares compare-at prices with AI agents. On by default for most stores, and it can be turned off. Shopify Changelog, 9 Oct 2026: compare-at prices for agentic channels

  4. 13 Oct 2026

    Shop MCP

    A hosted server at mcp.shop.com: one connection for Catalog search, cart and Shop Pay checkout. For developers of agents and apps. Shopify.dev Changelog, 13 Oct 2026: Shop MCP

  5. Fall ’26

    Knowledge base and eligibility check

    Merchants approve the facts agents repeat about the store and can see why products aren’t in Catalog. UCP adds /buy and /ask endpoints. Shopify Editions Fall ’26

What to do

Practical steps
for merchants.

Most of these improve the store even without AI channels. None of them promises visibility or sales; each channel ranks offers by its own rules.

  1. 01

    Check Sales channels > Agentic

    See whether the store is eligible, which channels are on and whether direct checkout is allowed. The channel decision is yours.

  2. 02

    Decide by market

    Selling only in Czechia and the EU? Then the AI channels in Agentic Storefronts don’t reach your shoppers today, and preparing is enough. Selling to the US, Canada or the UK too? Then it’s worth going through the channels one by one.

  3. 03

    Fill in product data

    Accurate titles, variants, images, categories and properties in metafields. An agent chooses from the data it can see; the Bohemian Coffee House case study shows a data model in practice.

  4. 04

    Complete policies and the knowledge base

    Terms of service, privacy and returns are required for every channel. The knowledge base then holds the facts an agent repeats about your store.

  5. 05

    Check prices and discounts

    For most stores, compare-at prices are shared with agents automatically. If you use them as a manufacturer’s recommended price, check the setting. The EU also has rules on how discounts are shown, so a struck-through price must reflect reality.

  6. 06

    Test the theme and checkout

    Custom theme code, checkout extensions and apps can affect what a browser agent sees. Worth testing before a larger change.

  7. 07

    Measure before you invest

    AI channel performance is in the Agentic settings. Decide on next steps from real visits and orders.

FAQ

What people
ask.

Do I need to switch anything on for agentic commerce on Shopify?

For eligible stores, Agentic Storefronts is active by default. It is still worth checking Sales channels > Agentic to see which channels are on and whether direct checkout is allowed.

Will shoppers in Czechia see my store in ChatGPT?

Not through Agentic Storefronts yet. According to Shopify, ChatGPT requires selling to customers in the US, so does Copilot, and Google serves the US, Canada and Australia. AI tools can still find your site by ordinary web crawling.

Do I need Shopify Plus?

No. Shopify ties Agentic Storefronts to store and product eligibility and to each channel’s requirements, not to the Plus plan.

What is the difference between MCP, UCP and WebMCP?

MCP is a general way for AI to call tools. UCP is a commerce standard for catalog, cart, checkout and orders that can run over MCP. WebMCP is a proposed web standard that lets a page offer tools to an agent directly in the browser.

Will better data guarantee more sales?

No. Good data improves the chance that a channel understands your offer correctly, but each channel decides on display and ranking by relevance, popularity and its own rules.
