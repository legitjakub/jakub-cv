Molecules of Life: a new store for the German market

Czech natural cosmetics brand Levandulové údolí entered Germany under a new brand, Molecules of Life. The Czech side stayed on Shoptet, and a separate Shopify store was built for Germany. On the Appfleece project team I built parts of the storefront.

Client
Molecules of Life
Industry
Natural cosmetics and organic products
Platform
Shopify, Czech store stayed on Shoptet
Market
Germany, priced in euros
Duration
2 months
My role
Storefront development, Appfleece team
01

Starting point

The company needed to enter Germany while keeping its Czech store running. A separate Shopify store supported the expansion, with its own identity, localised content and content management through Shopify.

02

Project scope

  • A new brand for a new marketA separate Molecules of Life brand and store identity for international customers.
  • Three product templatesLayouts for organic products, botanicals, and stays with treatments.
  • Content without codeMetaobjects and metafields, so the team edits copy and sections from the admin.
  • The Knowledge Garden blogEducational articles on lavender, cosmetics and sustainable practice.
  • German localisationEuro pricing, German payment methods, tax setup and legal texts.
  • Handover and trainingA series of sessions so the client team could run the store itself.
03

My contribution

I developed product pages and the mobile interface, adapting the theme to present a larger catalogue clearly.

04
