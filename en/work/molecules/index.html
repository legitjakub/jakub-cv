Molecules of Life: a new store for the German market

Molecules of Life is the German face of the Czech brand Levandulové údolí. At home the brand still sells through Shoptet; for Germany it got a new name, its own look and a separate Shopify store. In the Appfleece team I built parts of its storefront.

Client
Molecules of Life
Industry
Natural cosmetics and organic products
Platform
Shopify, Czech store stayed on Shoptet
Market
Germany, priced in euros
Duration
2 months
My role
Storefront development, Appfleece team

01

Starting point

The company needed to enter Germany while keeping its Czech store running. A separate Shopify store supported the expansion, with its own identity, localised content and content management through Shopify.

02

Project scope

  • A new brand for a new marketA separate Molecules of Life brand and store identity for international customers.
  • Three product templatesCosmetics, the Botanicals line and spa stays sell differently, so each group has its own template.
  • Content in the adminCopy and sections sit on metaobjects and metafields, so the team edits them itself.
  • The Knowledge Garden blogEducational articles on lavender, cosmetics and sustainable practice.
  • Set up for GermanyGerman copy and legal pages, prices in euros, and payments and taxes configured for the market.
  • Handover and trainingTraining for the client team, so they don’t need an agency for every change after launch.
03

My contribution

I developed product pages and the mobile interface, adapting the theme to present a larger catalogue clearly.
