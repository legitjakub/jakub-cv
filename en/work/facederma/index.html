Facederma: WooCommerce to Shopify Plus with B2B for salons

Facederma is a Slovak professional skincare brand. The project moved its Czech and Slovak stores from WooCommerce to Shopify Plus. As a developer, I worked on the migration and shopping experience for retail customers and salons.

Walk through the store

Recordings from facederma.sk, captured in November 2026. Switching pages starts the clip from the beginning.

01

Starting point

The original stores ran on WooCommerce. Moving to Shopify Plus involved data migration, a new catalogue structure and a URL redirect map. Salons also needed access to their own prices and wholesale purchasing within the same store.

02

Project scope

  • MigrationProducts, content, customers and the redirect map moved off WooCommerce.
  • B2B cataloguesA separate wholesale branch for salons, with their own prices and access.
  • Loyalty programmeEarning and spending store credit through the customer account.
  • NewslettersEmail flows configured and signup wired into the store.
  • Catalogue by skin concernCategories built around a specific need, plus filters and bundles.
  • Product pagesIngredients, reviews and related-product recommendations.
03

My contribution

I worked on the move from WooCommerce and development to the client’s brief: catalogue structure, B2B purchasing for salons, store credit, email communication and product pages.

04

What runs on the site today

  • The loyalty programme adds 2% of the amount paid for eligible products as store credit after dispatch, for use on a later purchase under the programme’s terms.
  • A “Facederma pre salóny” page with partner registration and wholesale pricing.
  • Categories by skin concern, such as wrinkles, acne, pigmentation or hydration.
  • Bundles that list the products they contain.
  • Newsletter signup and partner registration straight from the home page.
