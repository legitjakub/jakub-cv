Peace of Alm: a theme in the brand’s colours and type

Peace of Alm sells NATRUE-certified baby skincare made in the Austrian Alps. The store runs on Shopify on an off-the-shelf theme that we adapted to the brand in the Appfleece agency team. I worked on the project as the theme developer.

Client
Peace of Alm
Industry
Certified baby skincare (NATRUE)
Platform
Shopify, customised Wonder theme
Market
Czechia and Slovakia, priced in CZK
Range
Five products and two discounted bundles
Team
Appfleece
My role
Theme development, Appfleece team
01

Starting point

The brand has a strong visual identity: a periwinkle colour, Almík the marmot mascot and its own typefaces. The base theme needed to reflect the brand’s visual identity and the way its products are presented.

Ingredients, certification and suitability for sensitive skin need to be easy to find when choosing baby skincare. The labels highlight these details directly on the product page.

02

Project scope

  • Theme customisationThe Wonder theme adapted to a store with five products and bundles.
  • Matched to the brandColours, mascot illustrations and custom typefaces across the store.
  • Property labelsColoured labels on products, for example ingredients or certification.
  • Discounted bundlesProduct sets with the original and bundle price and a list of what is inside.
03

My contribution

  • Adapting the theme to the brand’s visual identity.
  • Custom typography: main headings in Gazpacho Black, body text in Avenir Next.
  • Product property labels, such as “NATRUE certified”, “Natural ingredients” or “Soothing”.
  • Discounted bundles: a product made of several items, with the original price, the discount and a list of what it contains.
04

Labels and bundles up close

The product page combines distinctive property labels with a clear list of the bundle’s contents. Customers can see the product features and included items without searching through a long description.
