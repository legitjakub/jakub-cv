Bohemian Coffee House: a coffee data model in a Shopify theme

The Rumburk roastery Bohemian Coffee House changed its visual identity, and its online store changed with it. Design came from Zdeněk Zeman, the build from the Appfleece team. My part was the theme, and working out how to store a coffee’s details in Shopify so the theme can use them on its own.

01

Starting point

The roastery sells coffees with different origins, processes and roast levels. Customers choose by taste and brew method. The store needed clear filters, links between the light and dark versions of selected coffees and straightforward product management.

Structured fields allow the same data to appear in product details, filters, collection cards and labels. The store team enters it once in the admin.

02

Project scope

  • Rebrand and new storeDesign by Zdeněk Zeman, build by the Appfleece team.
  • Online Store 2.0The older Motion theme became JSON templates with sections. The client now builds a new content page without a developer.
  • Coffee as dataOrigin, process, taste and roast are product metafields, not a paragraph in the description. The theme builds on them.
  • MobileThe coffee grid, filters and variant picker tuned for thumbs.
  • CZ, EN, DETranslated down to the text the theme generates: scale labels and brewing tips.
  • ShippingPacketa through Appfleece’s Globe app, with the pickup point chosen before checkout.
03

My contribution

  • Shopify theme development.
  • A metafield structure for coffee: origin, region, process, taste, roast and acidity as separate fields the theme can work with.
  • Roast and acidity scales drawn from the metafield values, in all three languages.
  • A collection card that uncovers the tasting notes on hover.
  • Collection filtering on the same metafields, with no hand-written tags.
  • A switch between the light and dark roast. The two roasts are separate products that act like variants on the page.
