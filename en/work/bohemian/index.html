Bohemian Coffee House: a coffee data model in a Shopify theme

Bohemian Coffee House is a family café and roastery in Rumburk, northern Czechia. Its rebrand came with a new Shopify store, designed by Zdeněk Zeman and built by the Appfleece agency team. I developed the theme and structured coffee product data as part of that team.

01

Starting point

The roastery sells coffees with different origins, processes and roast levels. Customers choose by taste and brew method. The store needed clear filters, links between the light and dark versions of selected coffees and straightforward product management.

Structured fields allow the same data to appear in product details, filters, collection cards and labels. The store team enters it once in the admin.

02

Project scope

  • Rebrand and new storeDesign by Zdeněk Zeman, build by the Appfleece team.
  • Online Store 2.0The Motion theme moved to sections, so the client’s team can edit the café, roastery and about pages independently.
  • 19 fields per coffeeOne admin record feeds the scales, filters, cards and labels.
  • Mobile layoutTwo columns of coffees, collapsible filters and larger variant buttons.
  • Three languagesCzech, English and German, including the scales and brew guides.
  • ShippingZ-BOX pickup chosen in the cart via Appfleece’s Globe app, and Packeta labels inside Shopify.
03

My contribution

  • Shopify theme development.
  • The coffee data model: each coffee’s details stored as product metafields, from country and region through process to flavour profile, roast level and acidity.
  • Roast and acidity scales on the product page, rendered straight from those fields.
  • Flavour profiles that appear on collection cards on hover.
  • Filters by brew method, origin and process, built on metafields.
  • Linking the light and dark roast of the same coffee: two separate products with a shared roast switch.
04
