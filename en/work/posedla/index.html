Posedla: custom saddles on Shopify Plus across four markets

Posedla makes custom 3D-printed cycling saddles and sells them worldwide from Czechia. I spent several months developing its Shopify Plus store, first with the Appfleece team and later directly with the brand.

Client
Posedla
Industry
Custom 3D-printed cycling saddles
Platform
Shopify Plus
Markets
Czechia, Europe, United Kingdom, United States
My role
Developer, with Appfleece and later directly
Duration
Several months
01

Starting point

The brand was growing from one market to global sales. It needed a store that handles several currencies with regional pricing, wholesale customers alongside direct sales and a link to its own ERP, while staying fast and easy to run.

02

Project scope

  • Shopify PlusRunning the store on Plus, including extended checkout customisation.
  • Theme redesignA custom design system and a theme built with load speed in mind.
  • Multi-currencyLocalised pricing and a geolocation redirect for visitors.
  • B2B branchA wholesale side of the store running alongside direct-to-customer sales.
  • ERP integrationThe store connected to the brand's own ERP system.
  • Posedla AcademyThe educational part of the site, with landing pages built on metaobjects.
03

My contribution

I worked on UX and UI improvements, theme development, integrations and selected parts of Posedla Academy, the brand’s educational platform.

My work with the Appfleece team continued into a direct collaboration with the brand.
