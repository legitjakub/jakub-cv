Posedla: custom saddles on Shopify Plus across four markets

Posedla 3D-prints made-to-measure cycling saddles from an imprint of the rider’s sit bones and ships them from Czechia to riders worldwide. I spent several months developing its Shopify Plus store, first directly for the brand and later with the Appfleece team.

Custom section

Saddle visualiser

A custom saddle is hard to explain in words. On the How it works page a visitor sets their sit-bone width and riding position and immediately sees how the saddle width changes and where the pressure moves. I built the section as part of the theme.

01What the customer sees

  • Saddle outline from aboveThe width follows the slider, with a pressure map from highest to lowest laid over the outline.
  • Pelvis on the saddleChanging the position tilts the pelvis. A label shows the angle: ≥ 65° for recreational riding, 46–64° for performance and ≤ 45° in the aero position.
  • Pressure focusA small outline with a line shows where the pressure moves: to the rear, the centre or the front.

02How it is built

  • Saddle width is calculatedSit-bone width plus an allowance for the position: 30 mm for recreational riding, 23 mm for performance and 17 mm for aero, kept between 130 and 170 mm.
  • 120 prepared drawingsThe outline exists in 40 widths, each with three pressure maps. The slider switches which layer is visible; the position swaps the whole set of drawings.
  • A custom sliderIt drags with a mouse or a finger and redraws once per browser frame, so the movement stays smooth.

03What took the most work

The layout. Three views, their labels and two controls have to fit side by side on a monitor and stacked on a phone, and the drawings have to keep the same scale across all 120 variants so the widths can be compared.

The phone therefore has its own composition: pelvis and pressure focus side by side at the top, the saddle with a width marker below them and the controls at the bottom. Both versions share one state, and the labels are translated with the store, so on posedla.cz the section is in Czech.

01

Starting point

Posedla now sells in Czechia, Europe, the UK and the US. Each market has its own currency and prices, wholesale partners buy alongside regular customers, and orders have to reach the brand’s own ERP. The store carries all of that and still has to stay fast and easy to run.
