Surprise Drinks: a brand site with mini-games for every licence

Besides a fruit drink, every Surprise Drinks bottle hides a collectible figure from a children’s licence. The Shopify site presents the brand to trade partners and gives kids mini-games, reached through a QR code on the label. I built it in the Appfleece team. It doesn’t sell online yet; for now it is there for presentation and play.

01

Starting point

The old site was dated. The brand deals mainly with distributors and retail chains, who need to understand the product, the licence portfolio and the flavours quickly.

The second audience is children. Every bottle carries a QR code, and behind it they should find games with the characters from the licence they are holding, with no sign-up and easy to play on a phone.

02

Project scope

  • Site on ShopifyOne theme for the European and American versions of the site.
  • Games via QR codeThe label code opens the games directly, with no login and touch controls.
  • Three games per licenceOne codebase for memory, word search and jigsaw. The licence supplies the characters, pictures and words.
  • Colours per licenceColours live with the licence, not in the game code. A new licence needs no developer.
  • Three markets, one storeEurope, Poland and the US from one theme. The American version has different licences and flavours.
  • Games as part of the themeWritten into the theme itself, no game app from the App Store.
03

My contribution

  • The brand site on Shopify: moving off the dated site, page structure and design.
  • Mobile mini-games: memory, word search and jigsaw with move and time counters.
  • Linking packaging to the site: QR codes on labels lead to the games for that licence.
  • Licensed content: pages and game sets for individual licences.
  • Localisation: language versions and markets.
04

From bottle to game

The QR code connects the physical product to the website: from the label to a licence page and then to the games. Visitors need neither an account nor an app installation.

05

How the mini-games work

  • From a licence page, kids jump straight into word search, memory or jigsaw in that licence’s colours.
  • The games are short and thumb-friendly: memory tracks moves and time, the word search hides character names, and the jigsaw gets simpler on phones.
  • All three are played live in the second video above.
