Surprise Drinks: a site for trade partners, with mini-games

Surprise Drinks makes fruit drinks in a patented bottle with a licensed collectible toy hidden inside. I moved the dated site to Shopify and rebuilt it as a presentation for distributors and retail chains, with mini-games for each licence.

Client
Surprise Drinks
Industry
Children’s fruit drinks with a licensed toy
Platform
Shopify
Audience
Distributors and retail chains
Licences
Including Paw Patrol, My Little Pony, Barbie, Hot Wheels, Sonic
My role
Site developer
01

Starting point

The old site was dated and did not speak to the people the brand mainly deals with: distributors and retail chains. They need to understand the product, the licence portfolio, manufacturing and safety quickly.

02

Project scope

  • Move to ShopifyContent migrated and the site rebuilt on a platform the marketing team can run itself.
  • Modernised designA new page structure replacing the dated presentation, built to read well on mobile.
  • Built for B2BLicence portfolio, flavours, manufacturing and safety instead of a product catalogue.
  • Custom mini-gamesMemory, word search and jigsaw, each with its own move, time and progress counters.
  • A set per licenceArtwork and game content matched to the specific brand, from Paw Patrol to My Little Pony.
  • Region switchMarkets separated straight from the site header.
03

How the mini-games work

  • Every licence has its own hub with three games: word search, memory and jigsaw.
  • The games count moves, time and progress, with artwork and content for that specific brand.
  • All three are played live in the video above.
04

My work

The move to Shopify, a new page structure and design, and building the mini-games.
