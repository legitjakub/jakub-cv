Surprise Drinks: a brand site with mini-games for every licence

Surprise Drinks makes children’s fruit drinks with a licensed toy hidden inside the bottle. Its new Shopify site combines a brand presentation with mobile mini-games reached through a QR code on the label. I contributed to development in the Appfleece team. At this stage, the site is for presentation and play rather than direct sales.

Client
Surprise Drinks
Industry
Children’s fruit drinks with a licensed toy
Platform
Shopify, one theme for the EU and US
Site type
Brand presentation and games, no sales
Markets
EU (27 countries, 14 languages), Poland, USA
Licences
Including Paw Patrol, Peppa Pig, Sonic, Barbie, Hot Wheels
Team
Appfleece, since November 2025
My role
Site developer, Appfleece team
01

Starting point

The old site was dated. The brand deals mainly with distributors and retail chains, who need to understand the product, the licence portfolio and the flavours quickly.

The second audience is children. Every bottle carries a QR code, and behind it they should find games with the characters from the licence they are holding, with no sign-up and easy to play on a phone.

02

Project scope

  • Site on ShopifyOne theme for the European and American versions of the site.
  • QR code on the bottleThe label code opens the games directly, with no login and touch controls.
  • Three games per licenceMemory, word search and jigsaw, with shared game logic and content for each licence.
  • Colours per licenceEach licence has its own colour scheme, and the team adds a new one with a single admin entry.
  • Three marketsThe EU for 27 countries in 14 languages, Poland with its own copy, and the US with its own licences and flavours.
  • Games in the themeThe games run inside the theme, with no third-party apps.
03

My contribution

  • The brand site on Shopify: moving off the dated site, page structure and design.
  • Mobile mini-games: memory, word search and jigsaw with move and time counters.
  • Linking packaging to the site: QR codes on labels lead to the games for that licence.
  • Licensed content: pages and game sets for individual licences.
  • Localisation: language versions and markets.
04

From bottle to game

The QR code connects the physical product to the website: from the label to a licence page and then to the games. Visitors need neither an account nor an app installation.

How the mini-games work

  • Every licence has its own hub with three games: word search, memory and jigsaw.
  • Memory counts moves and time, word search hides character names in every direction, and the jigsaw has fewer pieces on phones.
